# **App Name**: NutriVision

## Core Features:

- Image Upload/Capture: Allow users to upload food photos from their device or take a live picture using their camera.
- Food Recognition & Portion Estimation: Automatically identify the food items in the image and estimate portion sizes.
- Nutritional Analysis: Calculate detailed nutritional information (calories, protein, fat, carbs) for the identified food and portion.
- Personalized Diet Feedback: Provide personalized diet feedback based on the user's selected health goals, acting as a tool to provide advice and suggestions.
- Food Analysis History: Allow users to view a history of their past food analyses, sorted by date.
- Habit Tracking: Track the user's eating habits over time based on the analysis history.
- Healthier Choice Recommendations: Provide recommendations for healthier food choices over time, based on the user's habits and goals.
- User Login: Allow users to log in using a simple userID and Password

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Fresh green (#90EE90) to represent health and nutrition.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC) to provide a clean and calming backdrop.
- Accent color: Soft blue (#ADD8E6) to highlight key information and interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a modern, humanist sans-serif, providing a balance of readability and contemporary design.
- Use clear and simple icons to represent different food groups and nutrients.
- Ensure a clean and intuitive layout with a focus on visual clarity.
- Incorporate subtle animations, such as loading spinners and progress bars, to enhance the user experience.
